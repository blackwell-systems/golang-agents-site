Each finding comes from live runs recorded for [*Building AI Agents in Go*](/book/), on the models and dates the book names.
Small sample sizes are stated openly: a finding gives the number of runs behind each figure, and what a different sample might have shown.

New here? Start with [Workflow or agent, measured](/findings/workflow-vs-agent/) or the [error wording](/findings/error-wording/) page, which are the easiest to read; [the finished agent](/findings/the-finished-agent/) is the longest and assumes the rest.
On every page, the big number at the top is a headline, and the sentence under it is the finding.
