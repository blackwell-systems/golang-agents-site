Each design page names the problem, shows the design in Go, and says what the design cost in tokens, money, and turns across the book's recorded runs, with a short list of when to reach for it and when to leave it out.
Where a page uses goroutines, channels, or iterators, it explains them in a line, and the deeper passages sit in collapsible "Under the hood" panels you can skip on a first read.
